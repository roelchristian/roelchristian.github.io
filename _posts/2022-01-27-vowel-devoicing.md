I was watching [_Malmkrog_](https://letterboxd.com/film/malmkrog/){:
target="_blank" rel="noopener" } when I noticed that several of the speakers
displayed a remarkably consistent tendency to append a fricative-like
offglide—perhaps [ç], to my ear rather like the German _ch_ after front
vowels—whenever a word ends in [y], as in _perdu_. In attempting to identify
this phenomenon, I subsequently came upon the following article.
